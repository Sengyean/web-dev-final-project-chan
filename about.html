About Chan

Chan helps high school and university students in Cambodia find majors worth looking into.

How the matching works

Every major is tagged with related subjects and interests. When you choose yours, Chan counts how many of your choices each major matches and lists the majors from most to fewest matches.

Limits

Chan gives suggestions, not guarantees. University information may change, so please confirm details with the university.
